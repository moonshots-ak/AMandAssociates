Our International Practice

Cross-Border Service Offerings

01

India Entry Advisory

End-to-end support for foreign companies establishing India operations — entity structuring, regulatory approvals, FEMA compliance, and ongoing governance. Covers Liaison Office, Branch Office, Project Office and subsidiary structures.

02

FEMA & RBI Compliance

Comprehensive FEMA management — FDI reporting (FC-GPR, FC-TRS), ODI filings, ECB documentation, FLA returns and liaison with authorised dealer banks and the Reserve Bank of India.

03

Cross-Border Taxation

DTAA analysis, withholding tax optimisation, PE risk assessment, and international tax structuring for India-linked cross-border cash flows including dividend repatriation, royalties and service fees.

04

Transfer Pricing

TP policy design, benchmarking study, annual TP documentation and representation before the Transfer Pricing Officer during assessment proceedings.

05

IFRS / US GAAP Reporting

Group reporting for Indian subsidiaries of global groups — IND AS to IFRS conversion, accounting policy harmonisation, group pack preparation and consolidation workings.

06

Virtual Advisory Support

Cloud-enabled remote advisory for global businesses — real-time reporting, Virtual CFO services, and responsive support across time zones with structured governance and communication protocols.
