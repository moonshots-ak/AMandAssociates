Our Story & Philosophy

A Firm Built on Precision, Trust & Global Standards

Founded in 2018, Akshay Maheshwari and Associates (AMA) is a distinguished Chartered Accountancy proprietorship headquartered in Indore, Madhya Pradesh — the commercial capital of Central India.

Over years of focused practice, AMA has evolved into a sophisticated advisory firm trusted by cross-border businesses, foreign subsidiaries, domestic corporates, and high-growth enterprises. We combine technical regulatory mastery, commercial pragmatism, and sector-specific insight.

Our practice brings the rigour of Big-4 audit methodology with the responsive agility and dedicated relationship mindset of a high-end boutique advisory firm.

Our Purpose

Core Mission & Objective

Our objective is unwavering — to help our clients focus on and achieve their business and financial goals by providing services that are personalised, proactive, and tailored to meet their unique requirements.
