Our Industry Expertise

Deep Sector Knowledge. Precise Advisory.

Every industry has its own accounting treatment, tax obligations and regulatory landscape. AMA brings sector-specific expertise to every client engagement — not generic advice.

Real Estate & Construction

Project finance, GST on works contracts, land taxation, RERA compliance and developer accounting including JDA structures.

Healthcare

Hospital accounting, GST on pharmaceutical supplies, medical equipment depreciation, trust compliance and NABH-related advisory.

Manufacturing

Cost accounting, ITC optimisation, factory audit, transfer pricing for component sourcing and inventory valuation under Ind AS.

E-Commerce & Retail

Marketplace taxation, TCS/TDS on digital platforms, multi-state GST registration and high-volume accounts payable operations.

Technology & SaaS

Startup structuring, ESOP compliance, FEMA for overseas subsidiaries, R&D incentives, DPIIT recognition and Software export incentives.

Financial Services

RBI regulatory compliance, NBFC audit, FDI structuring for financial entities, investment fund advisory and tax compliance.

Automobile & Dealerships

Dealer accounting, OEM reconciliation, workshop GST treatment and multi-location inventory management for auto dealership groups.

Startups & Venture-Backed

Fundraising compliance, investor reporting, cap table management, DPIIT recognition, ESOP valuation and statutory compliance.

Logistics & Supply Chain

Multi-state GST, freight tax treatment, warehouse compliance, fleet depreciation management and intercompany billing.

Professionals & HNIs

Complex personal taxation, wealth structuring, succession planning, trust management and NRI/foreign national advisory.

International Trading

Import/export GST, customs duty advisory, foreign exchange management, trade documentation and DGFT compliance.

Foreign Subsidiaries in India

India-entry support, FEMA ongoing compliance, group reporting under IFRS, repatriation planning and RBI liaison.
