Practice Functions 01–04

Core Financial Services

Audit & Assurance

Audits that go beyond compliance — delivering actionable insights

  • Statutory Audit under the Companies Act, 2013 — risk assessment, substantive testing and issue reporting
  • Tax Audit with detailed disallowance reporting and management commentary
  • Internal audits, management audits and systems studies — process efficiency and control robustness
  • Bank audits including concurrent audits and statutory branch audits — credit review and fraud risk
  • SOX Act documentation and internal control testing for US-listed multinational subsidiaries
  • Transfer pricing audits — benchmarking and comparables
  • Certifications for cross-border remittances

International Taxation & FEMA

Cross-border advisory for global investors & foreign subsidiaries

  • India-entry structuring — Liaison Office, Branch Office, Project Office or subsidiary evaluation
  • FEMA compliance: inbound/outbound investment reporting, FC-GPR, FC-TRS, FLA returns and ODI filings
  • FDI advisory — sectoral caps, approval routes, pricing guidelines and downstream investment regulations
  • DTAA analysis and withholding tax rate optimisation for international remittances
  • Certifications for dividend repatriation, royalty payments and service fee outflows
  • Transfer pricing strategy — policy design, benchmarking and annual TP documentation
  • POEM regulations advisory and repatriation planning for NRI and foreign national investors

Accounting & Bookkeeping

IFRS-aligned records with real-time visibility

  • Full-service accounting using Tally ERP, Tally Prime, SAP, QuickBooks and advanced Excel
  • Preparation and finalisation of Trading A/C, Profit & Loss A/C, and Balance Sheet
  • IND AS conversion — technical advisory for companies transitioning from IGAAP
  • Group reporting consolidation under IFRS and US GAAP for Indian entities within global groups
  • Monthly MIS reporting — actual vs. budget analysis, variance commentary and cash flow statements
  • Multi-currency account management, bank reconciliation and intercompany reconciliation
  • SOP design for finance operations and internal billing procedures

Taxation — Direct & Indirect

Comprehensive, optimised tax strategy and compliance

  • Income tax return filing for Individuals, HUFs, Firms, LLPs and Companies including complex structures
  • Advance tax computation, TDS compliance management, e-TDS and TCS return filing across all sections
  • Tax planning and restructuring advisory — optimising effective tax rates for all structures
  • Representation before Income Tax Authorities — assessments, scrutiny, CIT(A) appeals and ITAT
  • End-to-end GST compliance along with multi-state registration
  • Input tax credit optimisation, GST health checks and compliance reviews
  • Representation before GST authorities during audits and departmental proceedings
Practice Functions 05–08

Advisory & Specialised Services

Virtual CFO & MIS Reporting

Senior financial leadership delivered virtually and adaptively

  • Embedded Virtual CFO — C-suite level financial oversight and board-level reporting for SMEs & foreign subsidiaries
  • Monthly MIS dashboard — P&L analysis, cost centre reporting, variance commentary and KPI scorecards
  • 13-week rolling cash flow forecasting and working capital optimisation
  • Budget preparation, quarterly reforecast and annual financial planning aligned to business strategy
  • Investor reporting packages and board decks to international institutional standards
  • ERP implementation support — chart of accounts design and reporting hierarchy configuration

Business Advisory Services

Strategic thought-partnership for corporate and entrepreneurial growth

  • Strategic financial analysis and commercial evaluation of investments, acquisitions and opportunities
  • Financial modelling — three-statement models, DCF valuations, scenario and sensitivity analysis
  • Feasibility studies and Detailed Project Reports (DPRs) for bank approvals and investor decks
  • Business valuation — intrinsic value assessments for M&A, taxation and restructuring
  • Profitability analysis, performance benchmarking and KPI design aligned to industry metrics
  • Startup advisory — entity structuring, cap table management, ESOP design and fundraising compliance
  • SOP design and implementation across finance, operations and compliance functions

Corporate Law & Consultancy

End-to-end corporate structuring and statutory compliance

  • Business constitution advisory — Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC, Trust and Partnership structures
  • Incorporation of Indian companies and JVs for foreign promoters — name reservation to commencement
  • Annual compliance for Companies and LLPs — ROC filings, statutory registers and board documentation
  • Mergers, acquisitions, de-mergers and corporate restructuring — structuring, valuation and documentation
  • Shareholders' and JV agreements review from a financial and compliance perspective
  • Strike-off, winding up and liquidation advisory including NCLT filings and FEMA de-registration

Payroll, Modelling & Controls

Compliance precision and robust internal controls

  • End-to-end payroll processing — TDS on salary, Form 16 issuance, PF/ESI/PT compliance and EPFO representation
  • Expatriate payroll management — shadow payroll, DTAA benefits for foreign employees on Indian assignment
  • Integrated three-statement financial models with dynamic assumptions and scenario toggles
  • Project finance modelling — debt sizing, DSCR analysis and IRR computation for lender engagement
  • Working capital facility structuring — CMA data and bank-ready project reports for CC/OD/term loans
  • Internal control framework design — COSO-based risk identification and IFC assessment
  • Workflow design — process documentation, RACI matrix and SOP preparation for finance teams
