About the Firm

India's Boutique CA Firm With a Global Mindset

Founded in 2018, Akshay Maheshwari and Associates (AMA) is a distinguished Chartered Accountancy and international advisory practice headquartered in Indore — the commercial capital of Central India.

We combine Big-4 audit methodology with the responsive agility of a boutique advisory firm — trusted by cross-border businesses, foreign subsidiaries, domestic corporates, and high-growth enterprises.

Our Services

Eight integrated practice areas covering every dimension of financial, tax, and regulatory advisory.

Audit & Assurance

Statutory, tax, internal, bank audits and SOX documentation for listed and unlisted entities.

International Tax & FEMA

India-entry structuring, DTAA analysis, transfer pricing, FEMA compliance and FDI advisory.

Accounting & Bookkeeping

Full-service accounting using Tally, SAP, QuickBooks; IND AS conversion and IFRS reporting.

Taxation — Direct & Indirect

ITR filing, advance tax, TDS management, GST compliance and representation before authorities.

Virtual CFO & MIS

C-suite financial oversight, MIS dashboards, cash flow forecasting and investor reporting.

Business Advisory

Financial modelling, DPRs, business valuations, startup advisory and SOP design.

Corporate Law

Incorporation, ROC filings, M&A structuring, winding-up and NCLT advisory.

Payroll & Controls

Payroll processing, expatriate management, financial modelling and IFC assessment.
